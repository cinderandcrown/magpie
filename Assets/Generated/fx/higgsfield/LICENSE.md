# The Neon City Bandit — UI & VFX art license

These are original game assets generated by **Cinder & Crown** with **Higgsfield** for *The Neon City Bandit*, a 2D iPhone platformer.

- **Created:** 20 November 2026 (America/Chicago)
- **Studio:** Cinder & Crown (Cinder and Crown LLC)
- **Generator:** Higgsfield (Nicholas Will's connected Higgsfield workspace, Ultra plan)
- **Tooling:** Higgsfield MCP `generate_image_batch` (model `gpt_image_2_6`, shown in the catalog as GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst; job metadata also listed the internal model name `aurora-img-alpha`). Quality: medium. Output: transparent PNG (icons) and transparent GIF (VFX), then frames were extracted and packed into sprite sheets locally with Python/Pillow. No background-removal pass was needed; Higgsfield returned usable alpha.
- **Style references:** two gameplay stills from the current build (raccoon bandit in goggles and a purple hoodie; white dome-headed MOP cleaning robots; rainy neon city at night with cyan, magenta, and lime accents). Those stills were uploaded only as generation references and are not part of this asset set.
