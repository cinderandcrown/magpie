# The Neon City Bandit — full art set license

These are original game assets generated by **Cinder & Crown** with **Higgsfield** for *The Neon City Bandit*, a 2D iPhone side-scroller.

- **Created:** 20 November 2026 (America/Chicago)
- **Studio:** Cinder & Crown (Cinder and Crown LLC)
- **Generator:** Higgsfield (Nicholas Will's connected Higgsfield workspace, Ultra plan)
- **Tooling:** Higgsfield MCP `generate_image_batch` (model `gpt_image_2_6`, shown in the catalog as GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst). Character poses and MOP variants used reusable reference elements `NCB-Bandit` (`0bcbc202-a278-4a46-a9dc-df52aa7708cf`) and `NCB-MOP` (`9c5587e9-dc2e-4d4a-a576-e5efa9e73279`), created from the accepted hero idle images. Quality: high for the raccoon and all MOP sprites (1K, transparent PNG); medium for backgrounds, tiles, loot, hideout, map, story beats, and UI. Backgrounds, map, hideout, and story beats were generated at 2K 16:9 (2688×1520). Sprite sheets were sliced locally with Python/Pillow. Faint background speckles were cleaned locally; Higgsfield returned usable alpha on transparent assets, so no rembg pass was needed.
- **Style:** glossy cartoon, neon magenta / cyan / lime on dark, matching the earlier UI & VFX pack in `/workspace/ncb-art`. The raccoon design is a glossy upgrade of the current in-game bandit (olive utility vest, cream belly, black mask with a cyan tech lens, green mesh loot bag). The basic MOP matches the dome-headed robot from that pack's mop-death art.
- **Style / design references** (uploaded only as generation references, not part of this set): a cropped idle pose from the current game character sheet, and `mop-death-01.png` from the earlier pack.

### Tile slice names

Each district sheet is a 2×3 grid, sliced left-to-right, top-to-bottom into: `platform`, `rooftop`, `ledge` (one-way), `vent`, `fire-escape` or `ladder`, `crate`.

### Known weaknesses

- The four raccoon run frames are consistent and usable, but frames 01–03 are fairly similar; frame 04 is the clearest extended stride.
- The pounce pose is a crouched ready-pounce more than a mid-air diving stomp.
- Parallax far layers have distinctive center landmarks, so they may not tile perfectly horizontally. Mid and near layers were prompted to tile; treat seams as a possible need for a manual wrap or a second plate.
- Story beats 01 and 02 are multi-panel comic layouts rather than a single still. Story 03's mini-boss is a bit slimmer than the dedicated `mop-miniboss` sprite, but it is clearly a dual-squeegee fight.
- The golden trash-can lid reads slightly medal-like because of the front boss and handle.
- The hideout couch has a spare bandit-mask prop; the room itself is empty of characters.
