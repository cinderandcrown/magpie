# The Neon City Bandit — app icon options

Original app-icon artwork generated by **Cinder & Crown** with **Higgsfield** for *The Neon City Bandit* (Cinder and Crown LLC). These two variants use the official cyberpunk raccoon bandit mascot shared with *Trash Stack*.

- **Created:** 20 November 2026 (America/Chicago)
- **Studio:** Cinder & Crown (Cinder and Crown LLC)
- **Generator:** Higgsfield (Nicholas Will's connected Higgsfield workspace, Ultra plan)
- **Tooling:** Higgsfield MCP `generate_image_batch` (model `gpt_image_2_6`, shown in the catalog as GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst; job metadata also listed the internal model name `aurora-img-alpha`). Quality: high. Resolution: 2K (2048×2048), then downscaled locally with Lanczos to 1024×1024 opaque RGB PNG. Background: opaque. No Higgsfield reference element was used (the off-model `NCB-Bandit` element was avoided). Official character stills, the character sheet, the approved Trash Stack icon A, and the old Neon City palette icon were uploaded only as image references.
- **Character:** official cyberpunk raccoon bandit — brass goggles with cyan lenses, cyan back cables and canister, black hoodie with magenta trim, leather straps with brass gears, teal/lavender sneakers. Gray-and-white fur. Not the earlier cute cartoon raccoon, and not the off-model orange raccoon.
